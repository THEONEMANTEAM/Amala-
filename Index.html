Amala


Amala is a Nigerian dish made from yam flour or cassava flour. It is often served with various soups and stews.

Amala is a staple "swallow" food originating from the Yoruba ethnic group of southwestern Nigeria. It is a smooth, dough-like dish made from yam flour (known as elubo), cassava flour (lafun), or unripe plantain flour.

stew

Stew is a thick, flavorful sauce made from tomatoes, peppers, onions, and various spices. It is often served with Amala to enhance its taste.

Meat

Meat is a common accompaniment to Amala. It can be beef, goat, chicken, or fish, and is usually cooked in a rich sauce or stew.

Juice

Juice is a refreshing beverage that complements the flavors of Amala. It can be made from various fruits and is often served chilled.
